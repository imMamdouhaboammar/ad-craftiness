Privacy Policy

Ask Farouk About Creative Thinking · «صنعة الإعلان» ("Ad Craftiness")
Last updated: October 17, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how Ask Farouk About Creative Thinking (developed by Mamdouh Aboammar) handles information when you use the plugin in OpenAI ChatGPT, Codex, or compatible agent environments.

2. Data Collection and Usage

Ask Farouk About Creative Thinking is a local, skills-only agent plugin.

  • No Personal Data Collection: We do not collect, store, transmit, or sell your personal information, user prompts, generated creative ideas, or account identifiers.
  • No Remote Servers or Databases: The plugin operates entirely within the host AI sandbox (e.g. ChatGPT / Codex platform). There are no external tracking servers, telemetry beacons, or third-party analytic trackers.
  • No Cookies: This website and the plugin do not set or read browser cookies or local storage trackers.

3. Interaction with OpenAI Services

When you interact with the plugin within ChatGPT or Codex, your conversation text is processed by OpenAI under their respective terms and privacy policies. Please refer to OpenAI's Privacy Policy for information on how OpenAI handles conversational data.

4. Third-Party Links

The plugin and its documentation provide verified educational links to Farouk Elkady's official channels (YouTube, Behance, Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, TikTok). If you visit these third-party platforms, their independent privacy policies govern your interactions.

5. Security

All plugin assets and instructions are distributed as static files under the MIT open-source license. The package contains no executable binaries, background network workers, or external network calls.
